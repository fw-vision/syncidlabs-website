# ADR 0003 - Trilingual, sovereignty-first, China dimension internal

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-07-29

## Context

SyncID's audiences are Canadian civic government (Councillor Ho, Mayor Scarpitti), capital partners, and venture founders. Markham has a large Chinese-Canadian community. SyncID's financing dimension includes a Canada-China dual-campus and Panda Bond / digital-RMB mechanisms that are sensitive and must never appear publicly.

## Decision

- **Trilingual as language-native registers, not translations.** Canadian English (primary sovereignty + credibility register), Quebec French (national-language legitimacy, genuine francophone participation), Mandarin Chinese (serves the Chinese-Canadian community as a Canadian-multicultural strength). Each carries the same actualizer spine and sovereignty confidence. FR/ZH are the deferred 20%.
- **Sovereignty-first public posture.** All public copy leads with Canadian sovereignty and verified evidence.
- **China dimension stays internal.** No China dual-campus, Panda Bond, or digital-RMB in any public page or the Mandarin copy. Those are project-level financing, vault-private (`_journal`).

## Consequences

- Public copy is sovereignty-first in all three languages; the Mandarin site is a Canadian-multicultural asset, not a China-facing surface.
- Any reference to cross-border financing in public copy is a leak - flag and remove.
- FR and ZH require human-native review before launch (not machine-draft).
